
## PAGE 4 — Consent Declaration (MOST IMPORTANT CHANGES)

[EXISTING - KEEP STRUCTURE, ADD THE FOLLOWING]

### CORRECTION 4: Add explicit "unticked by default" statement BEFORE the declaration items

**INSERT after "Please read each statement carefully and tick every box that applies to you. Your consent is voluntary, specific, informed, and unambiguous (per Section 32 of the Data Protection Act 2019). You may withdraw it at any time."**

> **Important:** All checkboxes below are **unticked by default**. You must actively tick each box to give consent. No box is pre-ticked. You may tick some boxes and leave others unticked — consent for one purpose is never treated as consent for another. Refusing or withdrawing consent for any optional purpose (Item 4) will not affect your ability to use UniHub. There is no detriment for refusing or withdrawing consent.

### CORRECTION 5: Update Item 3 (Core Consent) to reference separate purposes

**REPLACE:**

```
3. Core Consent: I consent to UniHub collecting and processing my personal data as described in Section 3 for the purposes in Section 4.
```

**WITH:**

```
3. Core Consent — separate purposes (tick each separately):
   3a. Identity Verification: I consent to UniHub collecting and processing my personal data (full name, email, phone, WhatsApp number, national ID/passport) to verify my landlord/agent account before any listing is published. [Lawful basis: Consent s.32 + Legitimate Interest s.30(1)(f)]
   3b. Listing Publication: I consent to UniHub publishing the property listings I submit (title, location, category, price, deposit, photos, amenities) so students can find housing. [Lawful basis: Legitimate Interest s.30(1)(f)]
   3c. Account Administration: I consent to UniHub processing my registration data, consent record, and review notes to administer my account and contact me about my listings and service changes. [Lawful basis: Consent s.32]
```

### CORRECTION 6: Update Item 4 label to be explicit about optionality

**REPLACE:**

```
4. Public Contact Details: I consent to my WhatsApp number and business email being published on my property listings so students can contact me directly.
```

**WITH:**

```
4. Public Contact Details (OPTIONAL): I consent to my WhatsApp number and business email being published on my property listings so students can contact me directly. [Lawful basis: Consent s.32 — you may decline and still use UniHub]
```

### CORRECTION 7: Update Item 7 (Withdrawal Right) to reference in-app withdrawal

**REPLACE:**

```
7. Withdrawal Right: I understand that I may withdraw my consent at any time by emailing hello@unihub.ke, which will close my account and remove my listings.
```

**WITH:**

```
7. Withdrawal Right: I understand that I may withdraw my consent at any time — either by using the "Withdraw" button on the UniHub consent screen (/consent) or by emailing hello@unihub.ke. Withdrawing consent will close my account and remove my listings, but does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before withdrawal (s.32(3) DPA 2019).
```

### CORRECTION 8: Update Item 8 (International Transfer) to be more specific

**REPLACE:**

```
8. International Transfer: I understand that my data is processed on servers outside Kenya (Vercel in the US, GitHub in the US) under Standard Contractual Clauses as safeguards.
```

**WITH:**

```
8. International Transfer: I understand that my data is processed on servers outside Kenya — hosting by Vercel (US East / iad1 region) and source control by GitHub (US) — under Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) incorporated in their Data Processing Addenda, supplemented by data minimisation (only public business listings and technical logs), encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+), and access controls (2FA, branch protection). No special category data is transferred.
```
