# Banned words and phrases

Replace with concrete alternatives. Do not use these in site copy.

## Vocabulary

delve, tapestry, landscape (figurative), testament, vibrant, pivotal, crucial,
intricate, meticulous, bolster, garner, underscore, interplay, multifaceted,
nuanced (filler), foster, leverage (verb), utilize, commence, facilitate,
encompass, paramount, groundbreaking, cutting-edge, game-changing, transformative,
revolutionize, seamless, robust (non-engineering), comprehensive (self-praise),
endeavor, aforementioned, harnessing, spearheading, navigating (figurative),
showcasing, highlighting, emphasizing, enhancing, unprecedented, remarkable,
stunning, profound, synergy, pain points, value proposition (casual),
moving forward, touch base, circle back, rest assured, elevated (as filler),
curated (as filler), immersive (as empty hype)

## Phrases

- "In today's [adjective] world..."
- "It's worth noting that..."
- "Let's dive in" / "Let's delve into"
- "At its core..."
- "When it comes to..."
- "Not just X, but Y"
- "It's not just about X — it's about Y"
- "From X to Y" as a range opener
- "At the end of the day"
- "The bottom line is"
- "Take it to the next level"
- "Unlock the power of"
- "Elevate your"
- "Streamline your"
- "Bridge the gap"
- "In conclusion"
- "Whether you're a student or a parent..." (empty dual address)

## UI meta copy (never on a marketing site)

- "Loop" / "scroll play" / "autoplay when in view"
- "Tap controls to unmute"
- "Begin the journey" as instructional chrome
- "Full clips loop while you scroll"
- "Watch first, then shortlist" as process narration
- Explaining reduced-motion or animation behavior in page body

## Punctuation

- Em dash (`—`) max one per page of marketing copy; prefer none
- En dash in titles is fine sparingly, or use `·`
- No exclamation stacks
