# UniHub premium marketing homepage

## Goal
Build a polished, dark single-page homepage that moves Kenyan students and parents from trust to browsing verified housing, while giving landlords a clear secondary path to list property.

## Page structure
1. Sticky translucent navigation with UniHub wordmark, section links, and Browse Listings CTA.
2. Full-height image-led hero with the supplied headline, safety message, CTA pair, and no-account trust strip.
3. Four-step journey from finding a home to chatting with an agent.
4. Three trust pillars: verified listings, renter-focused housing, and direct contact.
5. Six realistic featured property cards spanning hostels, bedsitters, rooms, and shared apartments.
6. Campus-location teaser with university chips and a restrained dark map-style visual.
7. Anti-scam statement with clear vetting language and factual, non-invented proof points.
8. Landlord/agent section with benefits and property-listing CTA.
9. Final browse CTA and compact multi-column footer with WhatsApp support.

## Visual direction
- Match the requested near-black, premium marketplace style using Space Grotesk and Inter.
- Use semantic design tokens for all colors, glass surfaces, borders, shadows, and glow.
- Generate cohesive East African campus-adjacent housing imagery for the hero and listing cards.
- Keep motion restrained: entrance stagger, image movement, CTA shine, and card lift, with reduced-motion support.
- Preserve rounded-3xl cards and high-contrast white pill CTAs while maintaining generous spacing on mobile.

## Technical details
- Implement at `/` in the existing TanStack Start app.
- Use accessible semantic sections, anchors, labels, focus states, and mobile navigation.
- Keep supplied destination links as placeholders: `/listings`, `/about`, `/contact`, and `/list-property`.
- Add page-specific title, description, Open Graph, and Twitter metadata.
- Verify desktop and phone layouts, navigation behavior, links, and current build health.
